Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

The short version
  • Everything you do in Maia Lune is saved only on your phone.
  • There is no account, and we never upload or share your information.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking in the app.
  • You can delete everything at any time from the Me page.

Who we are

Maia Lune is a puzzle game for Android. In this policy, "we" and "us" means the makers of Maia Lune. You can reach us at play@maialune.com.

What the app keeps, and where

To remember where you are, Maia Lune saves the following on your phone only:

  • your game progress, stars, rewards and Shine
  • your outfits, clothes and room
  • your journal entries and reflections
  • your settings (sound, music, flashing and vibration)
  • the name you choose to be called, if you add one (it's optional)
  • your answer to the 18+ question when you first open the app
  • a PIN, if you choose to lock the app (it is stored scrambled, never as the number itself)

This information never leaves your phone. We can't see it, and nobody else can either. The app does not ask for permission to use the internet, and it is not included in Android's automatic cloud backup.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • It does not use your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location.
  • It does not show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • It does not include analytics or tracking tools.
  • It does not sell or share any information about you.

Vibration

The app asks for Android's vibration permission so your phone can give a gentle buzz on bigger matches. It's used for nothing else. You can reduce it in the app's sound settings.

Purchases

Maia Lune is free to play. Some optional extras may be offered for purchase. All payments are handled by Google Play: we never see or store your card or payment details. Google tells the app only that a purchase was made, so it can unlock what you bought on your phone. Google's handling of your payment is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Google Play

Because the app is downloaded from Google Play, Google may collect information under its own policies, for example when you install the app or if it crashes. We may see summary reports from Google Play, such as how many people installed the app or how often it crashed. These reports don't identify you.

Deleting your information

Go to the Me page and choose Delete all my data. This removes your progress, rewards, journal and outfits from your phone straight away. Uninstalling the app removes them too. Because nothing is stored anywhere else, there's nothing for us to delete on our side.

Age

Maia Lune is meant for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children.

This website

This site uses no cookies, analytics or trackers, and its fonts are stored on the site itself. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, and GitHub may record visitors' IP addresses for security, as explained in the GitHub Privacy Statement. If you email us, we use your email only to reply to you.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email play@maialune.com.
