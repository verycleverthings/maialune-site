Help and support

Hello! Here are answers to the questions people ask most. If yours isn't here, email us and we'll help.

Playing

How do I play?

Slide a tile onto the tile next to it to swap them. Line up 3 or more of the same tile in a row or column and they clear. Collect the tiles shown in the goal at the top before your moves run out.

For a quick guide with little demos, tap the ? button on the Home screen.

What are power-ups?

Match 4 tiles to make a Sparkle, which clears a whole row or column. Match 5 for a Glow. Make an L or T shape for a Burst. Swap a power-up to set it off, and swap two together for a bigger effect.

I'm stuck on a level

That's alright: just try again, there's no penalty. A few things that help:

  • Look for moves that make power-ups rather than plain matches.
  • Work near the bottom of the board, where new tiles can drop in and make free matches.
  • If you run out of moves, you can use Shine for a few extra moves.

Every level can be won without buying anything.

Why does the journey pause after 7 new levels a day?

Each level has a small lesson, and a pause gives them time to sink in. You can still replay any level you've finished to earn more stars, and visit the dressing room, events and your journal. New levels open again the next day.

Settings

How do I change sound, music or vibration?

Tap the music button at the top of the Home screen. You can turn sound effects and music on or off, and reduce flashing and vibration. Volume sliders are on the Me page.

I forgot my PIN

Tap Forgot your PIN? on the lock screen. Because your information is kept only on your phone, there's no way for us to recover it, so this starts the app afresh and clears your progress.

Your progress and data

Can I move my progress to a new phone?

Not at the moment. Your progress is saved only on the phone you play on, so it isn't uploaded or backed up anywhere. Uninstalling the app removes it too.

How do I delete my data?

Go to the Me page and choose Delete all my data. Everything is removed from your phone straight away. Read more in our privacy policy.

Purchases

Do I have to pay?

No. Maia Lune is free to play, with no ads. Optional extras may be offered, and you'll always see exactly what you get before you buy.

How do I ask for a refund?

Purchases are made through Google Play, so refunds are handled by Google. Follow Google Play's refund steps. If something you bought didn't appear in the game, email us as well and we'll help.
