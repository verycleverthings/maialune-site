A little more you, one level at a time

Maia Lune is a calm match-3 puzzle game. Match tiles, learn small confidence skills with your mentor Paisley, and dress her for every step of the journey.

Coming soon to Google Play

For Android. For adults (18+).

What you'll find

Short, cosy levels that fit into a cup of tea, with something kind to take away from each one.

Match and grow

Swap tiles, make matches and set off sparkling power-ups. Each level brings a short, practical tip on confidence, voice, movement or style.

Dress Paisley

Earn Shine as you play and choose outfits, hair and accessories. Take on style challenges and decorate a room of your own.

Stories and seasons

Meet Paisley's friends, read their stories and visit seasonal events with pieces to collect through the year.

Our promise

  • No ads. Ever.
  • No paid random draws. If something costs money, you see exactly what you get.
  • Every level can be won without buying anything.
  • Your progress stays on your phone. No account, and nothing is uploaded. See our privacy policy.
  • Gentle on you. Turn off sounds or music, and reduce flashing and vibration, at any time.
